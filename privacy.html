Privacy Policy

NXF - NexGen Findr · Effective date: 1 November 2026

NXF - NexGen Findr ("NXF", "we", "us") is an app that helps people recover lost items, buy and sell, and find services nearby. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Name and email addressTo create and secure your account and to contact you about it.
Password (kept by Firebase Authentication; we never see it) and a security question with a one-way scrambled (hashed) answerTo log you in and let you reset your password.
Your posts: category, description, colour, brand, price, dates and times, optional phone number, area and map location, and search radiusTo show your post to the right people and to match it with other posts.
Photos you add to a postTo help identify items and to help matches decide whether a post is the right one.
Messages you send in a match chatTo let matched people talk to each other.
Location you choose on the mapTo place your post in an area and find nearby posts. We do not track your location in the background.
Notification token and basic usage counts (for example daily post limits)To send you match and message alerts and to prevent misuse.

2. How matching and AI work

To find matches, the text of your post and, when you add them, small versions of your photos are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini, with OpenRouter as a backup). The AI returns a match score and a short reason. We do not use your posts or photos to train our own models. The AI providers process the content under their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Who can see what

  • Only you can see your own posts and matches. Other people cannot browse posts.
  • When you are matched with someone, they can see the details of your matched post, such as the description, photos, area and any phone number you chose to add. Exact addresses are not shown.
  • Messages in a chat are visible to the two people in that match.

4. Services that help us run the app

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores your account, posts, matches and chats.
  • Cloudinary stores photos.
  • Cloudflare Workers runs our matching server.
  • Google Gemini API and OpenRouter score matches (see section 2).
  • Google Maps Platform shows maps and finds places.

These providers may process your data on servers in other countries. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show you advertising based on it.

5. How long we keep it

  • A post stays active for 60 days unless you extend, resolve or delete it.
  • Photos are removed from storage 30 days after their post is resolved or deleted.
  • Your account, profile and posts are kept until you delete your account.
  • Backups and logs held by our providers may keep copies for a short additional period.

6. Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can edit or delete any of your posts in the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This removes your login, profile and posts. Your photos are removed from storage after 30 days. Matches are closed, and messages you already sent stay visible to the other person in that chat.
  • To ask for a copy of your data or for help with deletion, email us at the address below.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access to it is limited by account rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share sensitive details in posts or chats.

8. Children

NXF is for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18 you may use it only with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Optional donations

The app may offer an optional donation link that opens your own UPI app. Payments are handled by your bank or UPI app, not by us, and we do not store payment details.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
